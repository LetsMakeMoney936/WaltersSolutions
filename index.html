Core Services

Mobile RV Repair for Real-World Problems

From roof leaks to mystery 12-volt issues, Walter's Mobile Solutions is built around the jobs RV owners actually face. Tap any service to see details.

Roof & Structure
Roof Repair, Resealing & Leak Tracking
Soft spots, sagging, or stains inside? The water is tracked to the source and sealed correctly.
Complete roof inspections, patching and sealing, vent and skylight resealing, awning and trim sealing, and long-term maintenance guidance to prevent future damage.
Electrical
12V & 120V Electrical Diagnostics
Lights flickering, batteries not charging, or breakers tripping for no reason?
House 12V systems, converters, basic inverter troubleshooting, shore power issues, GFCI trips, fuses, wiring faults, and battery health checks – with a focus on safe, clean fixes.
Water & Plumbing
Plumbing Leaks & Water System Issues
Leaks under sinks, soft floors, or pumps cycling non-stop are located and corrected.
PEX and fittings, water pump diagnosis, faucets, toilets, tank valves, and winterization and de-winterization support.
Slides & Leveling
Slide-Out & Structural Adjustments
Binding slides, bad seals, or that “something doesn’t look right” feeling when extending.
Visual inspection, seal checks, minor adjustments, lubrication points, and identifying issues before they turn into full failures.
Chassis & Running Gear
Brakes, Hubs & Suspension Checks
Uneven tire wear, strange noises, or floaty handling on the way to the campsite.
Visual inspections of brakes, hubs, bearings, and suspension components, plus recommendations to keep the rig safe on Texas roads.
Interior & Exterior
Cabinetry, Doors & Cosmetic Repairs
Sagging doors, loose trim, warped panels, and general wear are tightened up.
Cabinet adjustments, trim re-attachment, hardware swaps, and minor wall, floor, and door repairs that make the RV feel solid again.
Battery & Charging
Battery & Charging System Health
Dead batteries after a weekend or confusion about the power setup?
Battery testing, cable and lug checks, basic charge circuit diagnostics, and plain-English explanations of what the system can handle.
Inspections
Pre-Trip & Pre-Purchase Checks
Planning a long haul or looking at an RV near Nacogdoches?
Visual inspections of critical systems, roof, frame, slides, and water intrusion points. You receive a clear rundown of what’s solid and what needs attention.
